# BodyCast

Forecast how a training routine and diet would change your body over two years, shown on a realistic 3D model.
Calibrated for South Asian men (Pakistan and India). Everything runs in the browser; your details stay on your device.

- Single static page: `index.html` (three.js loads from jsDelivr).
- Body model: MakeHuman and MPFB assets (CC0). three.js (MIT).
- Estimates only, not medical advice.
